01 — About

Owning the pipeline, raw pixels to shipped service

I'm a Machine Learning / Computer Vision Engineer based in Ås, Norway, currently finishing a PhD at NMBU where I build vision pipelines that quantify melanin pigmentation in farmed Atlantic salmon — detection, segmentation and feature extraction turning raw underwater imagery into analysis-ready datasets for a multi-institution welfare study.

Before research I worked across three ML teams in Paris: product taxonomy classification at INIT ML, document understanding for scanned financial records at BASIKON, and ensemble career-pathway models at GOJOB. That mix left me equally comfortable with messy annotation work, model architecture, and the unglamorous deployment layer — Docker, Flask/FastAPI, MLflow, rate limiting, monitoring.

Lately I've been extending that into GenAI: fine-tuning vision-language models (Qwen3-VL), multi-agent LangGraph swarms, and RAG systems over research literature.
